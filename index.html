FAQ

Good questions

What does my $4 pay for?

Sales from this collection fund BigoCoin's independent security audit, the first step on the road to listing. How any funds beyond the audit cost are used will be announced in the Telegram and Discord.

How do I get my 500,000 BIGO?

Each NFT from this collection is eligible for a one-time reward of 500,000 BIGO on BNB Smart Chain. Claims are not open yet. When they open, you'll claim on this page with the wallet that holds your NFT, and the date will be announced in the community channels first.

Where does the BIGO come from?

From a reserve wallet set aside for NFT holders: 0x577D663f97F70726ba34445877E79fC16022c879. It covers 500,000 BIGO for every NFT in the collection. Anyone can check it on BscScan.

Is the live tracker real?

Yes. It reads the collection contract on Polygon directly: NFTs sold = 9,998 minus the NFTs still held by the sale wallet. The dollar amount is NFTs sold × $4.

Does this NFT give Miner app perks?

No. Miner app mining bonuses come from the original BigoCoin NFT collection. This collection is a separate collectible that funds the audit and comes with the 500,000 BIGO reward.

Is a listing guaranteed?

No. The audit is a required first step, but listing decisions are made by CoinGecko, CoinMarketCap and the exchanges themselves. Buy an NFT because you want to support the project, not as an investment.
